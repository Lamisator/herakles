herakles-desktop installs Herakles together with WebKitGTK, so that the
Herakles window (/usr/lib/herakles/herakles-window) can show the desktop
interface without a web browser. It contains no programs of its own.

Open "Herakles" from the applications menu, or run: herakles tray --open
Guide: https://herakles.kloeck-it.de/guide.html
